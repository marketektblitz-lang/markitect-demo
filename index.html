Billing questions

How does billing work?+

Your plan is charged automatically to your card every month, or once a year if you choose annual billing. No invoices to chase.

Can I cancel?+

Cancellation terms are being finalized. You will get them in writing, in plain language, before you sign anything.

What about the one-time build?+

The build is a separate, one-time fee agreed before work starts. Once it launches, your plan keeps it hosted, updated and supported.

Do I need a plan?+

No. You can just buy the build. Most clients add a plan so someone is always looking after the software.
